Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Fairway FC is a free football trivia game, run by an individual in the UK as a personal, non-commercial project. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking cookies. We collect as little as we can.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, kit, sound and graphics settings, streak and badges are saved in your browser’s own storage so the game remembers them. They are never sent to us. This storage is strictly necessary for features you choose to use, so we don’t ask for cookie consent. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data for this site.

Scores and sharing

There is no public leaderboard and we don’t keep your scores. If you tap a share button, your result goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, X or Facebook) and that app’s own privacy policy applies.

Online rooms: the only data we handle

If you join an online room, we receive the nickname you type, the room code, a random ID made by your browser for that session, and your score so far. We use them only to show the other players in that room where everyone is. This is our legitimate interest in running a feature you have asked to use.

Room data is held only in the server’s memory, never written to disk or a database. A player drops out of a room’s list after 45 seconds without an update, and the room is cleared once everyone has left. Please use a nickname, not your full name. Offensive names are replaced automatically.

Who processes it

The game is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes room data and standard technical information (such as IP addresses) on our behalf to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare may process this outside the UK, including in the United States, under safeguards recognised by UK law. We don’t sell or share data with anyone else.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask what data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted, restricted, or to object to how it is used. Because room data disappears within minutes and everything else stays on your device, there is usually nothing for us to hand over or delete, but you can still ask.

If you’re unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to us first, and you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

Children

Fairway FC is suitable for all ages. It has no chat, no profiles and no way to contact other players, and the only thing other players can see is the nickname you choose in a room.

Changes

If this notice changes, we’ll update the date at the top.
