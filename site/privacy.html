Privacy

Fairway FC is a free football trivia game. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, kit choice, sound settings, streak and badges are saved in your browser’s own storage. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Scores

There is no public leaderboard and we don’t keep your scores. When you finish, you can share your result through your own apps (WhatsApp, X, Facebook and so on). Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Online rooms

When you join a room, the nickname you type, the room code and your score so far are shared with the other players in that room. This is only held in the server’s memory, never saved to disk. Players drop out of a room’s list after 45 seconds without an update, and the room vanishes once everyone has left. Use a nickname, not your full name.

Who else is involved

The game is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. Cloudflare doesn’t use your nickname or scores for anything else. If you tap a share button, the app you share to handles what you post under its own privacy policy.
